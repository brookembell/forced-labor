## Data Source

The weight conversion factors in the dataset `unit_conversions_final.csv` originally come from the following research article: 

Blackstone, N. T. & Conrad, Z. Comparing the Recommended Eating Patterns of the EAT-Lancet Commission and Dietary Guidelines for Americans: Implications for Sustainable Nutrition. Curr Dev Nutr 4, (2020). [https://doi.org/10.1093/cdn/nzaa015](https://doi.org/10.1093/cdn/nzaa015)
