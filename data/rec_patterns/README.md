## Data Source

The HUS, MED, and VEG recommended dietary patterns were taken from the [U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2020-2025](https://www.dietaryguidelines.gov/sites/default/files/2020-12/Dietary_Guidelines_for_Americans_2020-2025.pdf).

The PHD dietary pattern was taken from the following research article:

Willett, W. et al. Food in the Anthropocene: the EAT–Lancet Commission on healthy diets from sustainable food systems. The Lancet 393, 447–492 (2019). [https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31788-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31788-4)
