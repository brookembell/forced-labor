## Data Source

The original dietary intake datasets were downloaded directly from the CDC and USDA websites:

- [National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), 2015-2016 cycle](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/continuousnhanes/default.aspx?BeginYear=2015)
- [National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), 2017-2018 cycle](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/continuousnhanes/default.aspx?BeginYear=2017)
- [Food Patterns Equivalents Database per 100 grams of FNDDS 2015-2016 foods](https://www.ars.usda.gov/northeast-area/beltsville-md-bhnrc/beltsville-human-nutrition-research-center/food-surveys-research-group/docs/fped-databases/)
- [Food Patterns Equivalents Database per 100 grams of FNDDS 2017-2018 foods](https://www.ars.usda.gov/northeast-area/beltsville-md-bhnrc/beltsville-human-nutrition-research-center/food-surveys-research-group/docs/fped-databases/)
- [Food Patterns equivalents for foods in the WWEIA, NHANES 2015-2016](https://www.ars.usda.gov/northeast-area/beltsville-md-bhnrc/beltsville-human-nutrition-research-center/food-surveys-research-group/docs/fped-databases/)
- [Food Patterns equivalents for foods in the WWEIA, NHANES 2017-2018](https://www.ars.usda.gov/northeast-area/beltsville-md-bhnrc/beltsville-human-nutrition-research-center/food-surveys-research-group/docs/fped-databases/)
- [WWEIA Food Categories, 2015-2016](https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400530/apps/WWEIA1516_foodcat_FNDDS.xlsx)
- [WWEIA Food Categories, 2017-2018](https://www.ars.usda.gov/ARSUserFiles/80400530/apps/WWEIA1718_foodcat_FNDDS.xlsx)
