## Data Source

The forced labor risk scores in the dataset `fl_scores_final.xlsx` originally come from the following research article: 

Blackstone, N.T., Rodríguez-Huerta, E., Battaglia, K. et al. Forced labour risk is pervasive in the US land-based food supply. Nat Food 4, 596–606 (2023). [https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-023-00794-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-023-00794-x)

The original dataset can be accessed here:

Tichenor Blackstone, N. et al. Supplementary Information for: Forced labor risk is pervasive in the US land-based food supply. Harvard Dataverse [https://doi.org/doi:10.7910/DVN/LEVNP2](https://doi.org/doi:10.7910/DVN/LEVNP2) (2023).
