## Data Source

The waste and inedible coefficients in the dataset `waste_inedible_coefficients_final.csv` originally come from the following research articles: 

Conrad, Z. et al. Relationship between food waste, diet quality, and environmental sustainability. PLOS ONE 13, e0195405 (2018). [https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0195405](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0195405)

Conrad, Z. Daily cost of consumer food wasted, inedible, and consumed in the United States, 2001–2016. Nutrition Journal 19, 35 (2020). [https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-020-00552-w](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12937-020-00552-w)
